Move your mouse.
I’ll look.

Sidebar headshot follows the cursor across eight directions. Hover the face and I look straight at you. Click it for a spit-second funny face, then the site flips dark/light and the sweater swaps with it.

How to try it

  1. Wave the cursor around the page — the head tracks you.
  2. Click the face (or press Enter/Space while focused) to toggle theme.
  3. Watch the sweater color flip with the mode.
dark + white sweater light + black sweater

The eight directions

Generated from your real headshot with AI — same pose & lighting, only the gaze changes.

Why this works

Classic trick: pre-render discrete head angles, then pick the nearest one from the angle between the face center and the cursor. No 3D, no video — just 8 (×2 sweater) stills swapped on pointermove.

If you like it, this can drop into the live sidebar (or the v2 redesign) once you’ve signed off on the likeness / expressions.

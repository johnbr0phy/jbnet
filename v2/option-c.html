John Brophy · Builder & Product Leader · 2003 — 2026

Six rooms. Twenty‑three years of building.

For most of my career I was a design-led PM. I could see the product clearly, could tell you when a few pixels were off, but I needed a team to write the code.

Since December 2025 that stopped being true. I am, first and foremost, a builder — designing, coding and PMing at once. Idea in the morning, live by lunch.

Walk through. One plate per era, one number each. The full record is in the resume PDF, and what I'm building now is on Current Projects.
